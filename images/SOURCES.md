# Image sources

The images in this directory were selected from the Café Woodvine channels
provided and approved by the project owner.

- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/cafe_woodvine/
- Naver Place: https://m.place.naver.com/restaurant/1153262181/home

`hero-event-set.jpg`, `bulk-cakes.jpg`, and `celebration-cakes.jpg` are from
the official Instagram account. The remaining café and product images are
from the linked Naver Place page.

The `menu-*.jpg` files and other website JPEG files may be AI-assisted
photographic variations derived from owner-approved originals. The original
menu items, packaging, and café interior identity were retained while framing,
lighting, background, and editorial styling were refined using visual
references supplied by the project owner.
